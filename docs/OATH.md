# A Researcher's Oath

> **Build systems that can prove you wrong.**
>
> Prefer evidence over elegance.
> Prefer reproducibility over convenience.
> Prefer simple baselines over complicated assumptions.
> Measure before optimizing.
> Compare before replacing.
> Document before forgetting.
>
> When an experiment succeeds, understand *why*.
> When an experiment fails, understand *why* even more carefully.
>
> Never discard negative results—they define the boundaries of what is true.
>
> Freeze infrastructure when it is sufficient.
> Evolve only the hypotheses.
>
> Let every benchmark be reproducible.
> Let every figure be traceable.
> Let every conclusion be earned.
>
> **The data has the final vote.**
